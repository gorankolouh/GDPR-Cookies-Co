What this check looks at

This audit is a website-focused practical review. It should be treated as a starting point for identifying issues, not as a formal legal certification or legal opinion.

1

Cookies and consent

Review how the website asks for consent and how the consent experience relates to cookies and tracking technologies.

2

Privacy policy

Look at whether the privacy information appears to explain important processing details clearly and accessibly.

3

Legal pages and forms

Review legal information, contact forms and other points where visitors may submit personal data.

4

Third-party processing

Identify third-party technologies and services that may create additional data flows or require clearer documentation.

How the review works

The goal is to give a website owner a clear starting point: what appears to be working, what needs attention, and what to investigate next.

1. Start with the free check

Submit your website address and email. The initial review is designed to answer whether there are obvious areas that deserve attention.

2. Understand the findings

If issues are found, you receive a short description rather than having to purchase a report before seeing whether there is a problem.

3. Get the detailed audit

The €99 one-time report expands the findings, explains why each area matters and organizes suggested fixes.

4. Re-check after improvements

After making changes, the service includes one additional free check so you can see whether the reviewed areas have improved.

Who is this useful for?

Businesses, freelancers, agencies and website teams that want a practical outside review before handing findings to their developer, marketing team or legal adviser.
