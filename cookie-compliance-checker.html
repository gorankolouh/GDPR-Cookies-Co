What this check looks at

The presence of a cookie banner alone does not establish compliance. The wider setup, including technologies that run and the information provided to visitors, matters too.

1

Cookie consent banner

Check whether visitors are presented with a clear consent choice and whether the interface makes the available choices understandable.

2

Analytics and advertising

Look for analytics tools, advertising pixels and similar technologies that may process visitor information.

3

Third-party services

Embedded video, maps, chat tools, social widgets, fonts and other services can introduce additional data flows worth reviewing.

4

Before-consent behavior

One important question is whether relevant tracking or non-essential technologies appear to run before the visitor has made a choice.
